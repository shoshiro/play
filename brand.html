IIThe seal

Seven letters, drawn as a lyre.

  1. The keyed wheel. The 26 letters sit around a circle, but not in alphabetical order. Each letter moves seven steps from the last, which shuffles the alphabet into the brand's own sequence.
  2. The path. One stroke visits R, E, S, Y, T, A and L in turn, bowing toward the centre between letters. With seven letters on a seven-step wheel, the line draws a lyre, the oldest instrument of the recital.
  3. The terminals. The line opens with a small ring and closes with a bar, the way hand-cut seals always have. The seven letters are marked in gold on the tick ring.
  4. The brand's letter order.

IVThe programme

A recital has a programme. So does the brand.

You don't need to know yet what you'll make. The programme has one line for what you sell and one for what you share, and both count from the first piece.

VIIVoice

Say less. Name the material, the number, the date.

  • No exclamation marks, no emoji, no countdowns. Scarcity is stated, never shouted.
  • Numbers are exact: Nº 004, forty made, ships 3 October.
  • Things are "pieces" and "notes", never "content", "drops" or "merch".
  • Prices are shown plainly. The brand does not run sales.
  • Write like a letter to one person, not an announcement to a crowd.
